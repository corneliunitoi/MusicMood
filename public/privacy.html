Privacy Policy

MusicMood · Last updated: 21 November 2026

MusicMood is a personal music discovery app that plays music by mood. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Account details (Google sign-in): your name, email address and profile picture, provided by Google when you choose to sign in. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Guest sessions: if you continue as a guest, an anonymous account identifier is created for your device. No name or email is collected.
  • Listening activity: the tracks you play, how long you listen, and whether you finish, skip, like or dislike them, together with the mood and genre you selected.
  • Your preferences: favorites, upvotes, taste calibration (genre preferences and exclusions), genre-correction flags and artist requests.
  • Feedback you choose to send, including an optional name.
  • YouTube playlists (optional): only if you click “Connect YouTube”, the app asks Google for read-only access to your YouTube playlists so you can import them. This access is used only to list and import the playlists you choose, is kept in your browser for about an hour, and is not stored on our servers.

How we use it

  • To run the app: sign you in, remember your favorites and settings across devices, and recommend music.
  • To improve recommendations, for example by learning which genres and artists listeners enjoy or skip. Community signals such as upvotes are aggregated and do not reveal who cast them.
  • To keep the service working and safe, for example rate limits and abuse prevention.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

Where it is stored and who processes it

  • Supabase (database and authentication), hosted in the EU West (London) region.
  • Render hosts the web app.
  • Google provides sign-in and, if you connect it, YouTube playlist access.
  • YouTube: music plays through YouTube's embedded player, which is subject to Google's Privacy Policy and YouTube's Terms of Service.

Your choices and rights

  • You can use MusicMood as a guest without giving any personal information.
  • You can revoke MusicMood's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • You can ask us to export or delete your account and all associated data by emailing corneliunitoi87@gmail.com. We act on requests within 30 days.

Retention

We keep your data while your account exists. When you ask us to delete your account, your profile, favorites, listening history and preferences are deleted. Anonymous, aggregated statistics (such as a track's total upvotes) may remain.

Children

MusicMood is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, notify users in the app.

Contact
